About the project

Curiosity is the beginning.

MED by Eldana is an independent student science-communication project designed to make medical science more approachable. It shares learning resources about biology, disease mechanisms and emerging biomedical research, with references to help readers explore further.

This website is for educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Articles should be reviewed and updated as scientific understanding evolves.
